If you already maintain lists on **MyAnimeList** or **AniList**, you don't need to rebuild everything.

## Import flow

- Open the **Add** dialog
- Expand the **Import** section
- Choose MAL or AniList
- Sign in / enter username (depending on provider)
- Select what you want to bring in

## What gets imported

Typically:

- Your anime list (watching / completed / plan to watch)
- Your manga list (reading / completed / plan to read)

After import, you can tweak per-item settings as needed.
